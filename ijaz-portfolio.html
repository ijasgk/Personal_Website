Bridging Infrastructure & Velocity

Transforming fragile operational tasks into deterministic, self-healing software systems.

I am a DevOps and Infrastructure Engineer at AMD, working at the convergence of hardware verification and scalable cloud systems. My mission is building robust foundations where engineering teams can deploy code with total confidence, zero manual friction, and predictable uptime.
